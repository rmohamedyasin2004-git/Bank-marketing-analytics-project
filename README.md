# 🏦 Bank Telemarketing Campaign Analytics & Customer Subscription Modeling

## 📋 Project Overview
This project performs in-depth Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and statistical modeling on the UCI Bank Marketing dataset (45,211 customer records). The goal is to uncover behavioral patterns, demographic drivers, and campaign characteristics that successfully convert prospective clients into term-deposit subscribers.

## ⚙️ Key Analytical Workflow
- **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Handled missing/unknown values, removed duplicate rows, and performed strict data type validation across 17 multivariate attributes.
- **Feature Engineering:** Created derived behavioral features, grouped customer age brackets, and segmented campaign contact intensities.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Visualized distributions of customer account balances, age groups, call durations, and seasonal campaign success rates.
- **Statistical Summaries:** Computed central tendencies, variance, skewness, and IQR-based outlier analysis on core numeric metrics.

## 📈 Key Insights & Findings
- **High-Converting Segments:** Management and retired professional tiers exhibited higher baseline subscription tendencies.
- **Call Duration Impact:** Longer conversation durations strongly correlated with positive subscription conversions, emphasizing agent engagement quality.
- **Timing & Seasonality:** Campaign success rates varied significantly by month, highlighting optimal windows for telemarketing outreach.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SciPy, Statsmodels

## 🚀 Business Value
Empowers retail banking marketing teams to optimize resource allocation, target high-probability customer segments, and design data-backed telemarketing campaigns with reduced acquisition costs.
